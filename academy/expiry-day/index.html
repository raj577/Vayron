Expiry day in F&O: what changes and why

Every futures and options contract has an expiry date, after which it stops trading and settles. Expiry day often behaves differently from other sessions: option prices move faster, open interest unwinds, and the market can swing sharply in the final hours. Understanding why helps you avoid surprises.

The current expiry schedule

At the time of writing, NSE's equity derivatives expire on Tuesdays. NIFTY has weekly options, and monthly contracts expire on the last Tuesday of the month. SEBI's late-2024 rules limited each exchange to weekly options on a single benchmark index. If an expiry day is a trading holiday, expiry moves to the previous trading day. Exchanges and SEBI revise these rules from time to time, so always check NSE's official calendar.

How contracts settle

  • Index futures and options are cash-settled. No shares change hands; the difference is paid in cash based on the final settlement price.
  • Stock futures and options are physically settled. If you hold an in-the-money stock option or a stock future into expiry, you may have to take or give delivery of the shares, which needs the full cash or shares in your account. Brokers often square off such positions earlier or ask for extra margin.
  • Out-of-the-money options expire worthless. In-the-money options settle at their intrinsic value.

Why options move so fast on expiry

On expiry day, options have only hours of time value left. Two Greeks dominate:

  • Theta is at its most intense. Out-of-the-money options can lose most of their remaining value within hours if the price does not move towards them.
  • Gamma is at its highest for at-the-money strikes. A small move in the index can flip an option from almost worthless to deep in the money, so premiums can jump several times over, or collapse to zero.

This is why cheap expiry-day options look tempting. Most of them still expire worthless, and the few big winners are hard to pick in advance.

Open interest and max pain on expiry

As positions are closed or rolled, total OI falls through the day. Writers defend or abandon their strikes, so OI walls in the option chain can shift quickly. Some traders watch max pain on expiry day, but large flows and news regularly override it.

Futures on expiry

The futures premium converges towards the spot price as the day goes on, as explained in the futures basis lesson. Traders who want to keep their position roll it into the next contract before expiry, usually in the last few sessions of the series.

Extra safeguards on expiry day

SEBI and the exchanges have added measures for expiry-day risk, such as an additional margin on short index options on expiry day and removal of calendar-spread margin benefits on that day. These raise the capital needed to sell options on expiry. Check your broker's margin calculator, because requirements can change.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Buying many far out-of-the-money options in the last hour because they are cheap.
  • Selling options on expiry without understanding how fast gamma can turn a small loss into a large one.
  • Holding in-the-money stock options into expiry without the cash or shares for physical delivery.
  • Forgetting that transaction costs and taxes apply to exercised options too.

Frequently asked questions

Which day is F&O expiry on NSE?

At the time of writing, NSE equity derivatives expire on Tuesdays, with monthly contracts on the last Tuesday of the month. Check NSE's official calendar because rules can change.

What happens if I hold an ITM stock option on expiry?

Stock options on NSE are physically settled, so you may have to buy or deliver the shares. Brokers often square off these positions before expiry or ask for extra margin.

Why do options become so cheap on expiry day?

Almost all of their time value has decayed. Out-of-the-money options are priced low because there is very little time left for them to finish in the money.
