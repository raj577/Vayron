India VIX: the market's fear gauge explained

India VIX is a volatility index published by NSE. It measures how much movement the options market expects in NIFTY over the next 30 calendar days. Because volatility usually jumps when markets fall sharply, India VIX is often called the market's "fear gauge".

How it is calculated

India VIX is computed from the order book of NIFTY options, using the best bid and ask prices of near-month and next-month contracts across a range of strikes. NSE adapted the methodology from the CBOE VIX used in the US. The result is expressed as an annualised percentage. You do not need to calculate it yourself; what matters is what the number implies.

What high and low VIX mean

  • Rising VIX: uncertainty is increasing and option premiums are getting more expensive. Sharp rises often accompany sell-offs, elections, budgets or global shocks.
  • Falling VIX: the market expects calmer conditions. Options get cheaper and ranges tighten.
  • Very low VIX for a long time: complacency. Calm periods can end suddenly.

VIX and NIFTY usually move in opposite directions, but not always. Sometimes both rise ahead of a big event, as traders buy options for protection and for upside.

Why VIX matters for option traders

India VIX is essentially a blended implied volatility of NIFTY options. When VIX is high, option sellers collect larger premiums but face bigger potential moves; buyers pay more and need a larger move to profit. When VIX is low, buying options is cheaper but the market is pricing small moves. Many traders adjust strike distance and position size as VIX changes. The Greek that captures this sensitivity is vega, covered in option Greeks.

VIX and expiry ranges

Traders use VIX or the IV of the nearest expiry to estimate a range into expiry. For a short expiry, scale by the square root of the trading days left. Common misunderstandings

  • VIX does not predict direction. It measures the expected size of moves, up or down.
  • A VIX spike is not automatically a bottom. Fear can stay high or rise further.
  • VIX is about NIFTY. Individual stocks can be far more volatile than India VIX suggests. Check each stock's own IV.

Frequently asked questions

What is a normal level for India VIX?

India VIX has spent long periods between the low teens and the low twenties, with spikes far higher during crises. It is best compared with its own recent history rather than a fixed number.

Does a high India VIX mean the market will fall?

No. VIX measures expected volatility, not direction. It often rises during falls, but it can also rise before big events regardless of direction.

How do I convert India VIX into a daily NIFTY range?

Divide VIX by about 16. A VIX of 16 implies a typical one-standard-deviation daily move of about 1%.
