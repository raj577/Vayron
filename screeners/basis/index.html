Futures basis screener: premium and discount in NSE stock futures

The basis screener compares each stock future with its spot price, showing which trade at an unusual premium or at a discount.

Futures normally sit slightly above spot because of the cost of carry, so large premiums or discounts can point to strong positioning, dividends or heavy hedging.

Live figures appear only after you connect your own broker; until then the dashboard shows labelled example data for learning.
