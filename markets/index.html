Learn F&O the right way

Futures vs options

A future is an obligation to buy or sell at a set price on expiry. An option is a right, not an obligation. Buyers pay a premium and risk only that premium, while sellers collect it and take on larger risk.

Open interest

Open interest is the number of contracts still open. Rising price with rising OI suggests long build-up. Falling price with rising OI suggests short build-up. Reading both together beats reading price alone.

Option Greeks

Delta tracks how much the premium moves with the underlying, theta is daily time decay, vega is sensitivity to volatility and gamma is how fast delta changes. Option buyers fight theta every day.

Risk first

SEBI studies show most individual F&O traders lose money. Size positions so one bad trade can't hurt you, always use a stop, and never trade money you need.
